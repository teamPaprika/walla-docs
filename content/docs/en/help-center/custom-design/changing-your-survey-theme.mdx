## How to Change the Theme

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Click the Branding button
    Click the `Branding` button at the top of the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose background and button colors
    Pick from a range of theme colors to restyle your survey.
    <Callout type="warning" title="Warning">
If your button color is too close to your background color, the required marker or button text may be hard to see.
</Callout>
    
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose text and button text colors
    If you don't set the text color or button text color separately, they'll automatically adjust to fit the background color you chose.
    The colors of options and input fields will also automatically adapt to blend naturally with your chosen **text color**.
  </Step>
</Steps>


<Callout title="Tip">
If you're struggling with color combinations, try a color palette site (e.g., [2 Color Combinations](https://2colors.colorion.co/), [Color Hunt](https://colorhunt.co/)) to quickly build a balanced and natural-looking theme.
</Callout>
