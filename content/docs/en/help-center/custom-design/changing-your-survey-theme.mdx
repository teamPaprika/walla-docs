description: "Customize your survey's background color, theme color, and text color to match your brand or the tone you're going for."
---

## How to Change the Theme

1. Click the `Branding` button at the top of the editor.
2. Choose your background color and theme color to restyle the survey.
3. If the background color is very dark, text color automatically switches to white (#FFFFFF) to keep things readable.

<Callout type="warning" title="Warning">
If your button color is too close to your background color, the required marker or button text may be hard to see.
</Callout>
<Callout title="Tip">
If you're struggling with color combinations, try a color palette site (e.g., [2 Color Combinations](https://2colors.colorion.co/), [Color Hunt](https://colorhunt.co/)) to quickly build a balanced and natural-looking theme.
</Callout>
