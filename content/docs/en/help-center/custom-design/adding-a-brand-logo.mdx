## How to Add a Logo

1. Click the `Branding` button at the top of the editor.
2. Find the 'Add brand logo' option.
3. Upload your image file — and you're done!

## Where It Appears

- Your brand logo is shown below the submit button.
- The "Powered by Walla" logo that appears by default on the Free plan is hidden.

<Callout title="Tip">
Very large images may be cropped in the display area, so we recommend using a horizontal logo or a transparent PNG file.
</Callout>
