## Overall Survey Insights

- Total Views: The total number of times the survey was opened and viewed.
- Total Starts: The number of respondents who actually started answering.
- Total Submissions: The number of respondents who completed and submitted the survey.
- Average Completion Time: The average time it takes a respondent to finish the survey.
## Field-Level Analysis

- Track views and drop-off rates for each question. → See where respondents linger the longest or where they abandon the survey.
- You can also check each question's average response time to pinpoint confusing or time-consuming questions.
## Drop-Off Point Analysis

- Detect where respondents leave before finishing the survey.
  Use this information to improve the survey flow, simplify
  questions, and reduce drop-offs.
