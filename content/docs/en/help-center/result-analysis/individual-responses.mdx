---

## Viewing an Individual Response
1. Go to the Individual Responses tab for the project.
2. Click a response in the list to see every answer the respondent submitted.
  - Select `Show all questions` to view every question on one screen, including questions the respondent didn't actually answer.

## Managing Responses
- `Download Response`: Save the selected response as a PDF file.
- `Delete Response`: Permanently delete a specific response. (Deleted responses cannot be recovered.)
