## Viewing an Individual Response

1. Click the **Results** tab in your project, then select the **Individual Responses** tab.
2. Responses are displayed in full screen, starting from the most recent.
3. Use the left and right arrows to move to the previous or next response. The current position is shown at the top, such as "3 / 15".

- Turn on **Show all questions** to view every question, including those the respondent didn't answer.
- When a workspace member shares a specific response link with you, it will take you directly to this page.
- At the bottom, you can find a summary of the project name, response time, survey ID, and response ID for easy identification.

## Managing Responses

- **Copy link**: Copies the link to this Individual Responses page for the current response. Share it with workspace members who have permission to view responses so they can check it directly on this page.
- **Download PDF**: Saves the current response as a PDF file.
- **Delete**: Permanently deletes the response. Deleted responses cannot be recovered.

<Callout title="Tip">
You can also open a response detail view directly from the Response Sheet by clicking the row icon — without leaving the sheet. See [Response Sheet](/en/docs/help-center/result-analysis/response-sheet#viewing-individual-responses-from-the-sheet) for details.
</Callout>
