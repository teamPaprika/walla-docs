## Statistics

- An appropriate visualization is automatically applied based on the response type.
- Click `Response Filter` at the top to analyze only responses matching specific conditions (date range, an exact or partial match for a question’s value, etc.).
- Click `Download all questions as PDF` at the top to save the statistics for all questions as a PDF.
- Use the arrow next to `Download` to choose PNG or SVG and save an individual question’s statistics.
