- Click `Response Filter` at the top to analyze only responses matching specific conditions (by period, field value match/contains, etc.).
- Click `Download all questions as PDF` at the top to save every question's statistics as a PDF.
- Statistics show each question's results as charts (graphs) or tables.
- An appropriate visualization is automatically applied based on the response type.
  - Multiple choice: pie chart, vertical bar chart, horizontal bar chart, table
  - Short text: table, word cloud

- Individual questions can be downloaded as PNG image files.
