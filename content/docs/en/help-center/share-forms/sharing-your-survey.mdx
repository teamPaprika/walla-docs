---
title: "Sharing Your Survey (URL, QR Code, Embed)"
description: "Once you've built your survey, it's time to share the URL with respondents. Walla lets you share surveys in two view modes: standard view and grouped view."
---

## Sharing by URL

Copy the survey link and paste it into any channel you like — messenger, email, social media — to send it to respondents.

### Copying the URL
Click the copy icon next to the link to copy the standard view or grouped view URL to your clipboard.

<img src="/images/guides/share-forms/share-copy-link.png" alt="The copy icon highlighted in the survey link area of the share screen" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />

### Opening the URL in a new window
Click the arrow icon to open the survey in a new window and see exactly what respondents will see.

<img src="/images/guides/share-forms/share-open-new-tab.png" alt="The open in new window arrow icon highlighted in the survey link area of the share screen" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />

## Sharing by QR code

Turn the survey URL into a QR code so respondents can reach it easily from offline places such as posters, flyers, or your store.

### Opening the QR code
Click the QR code icon to view and download the QR code right away.

<img src="/images/guides/share-forms/share-qr-code.png" alt="The QR code icon highlighted in the survey link area of the share screen" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />

## Embedding the code

Copy the iframe embed code and insert it into your own website or landing page so respondents can take the survey without leaving the page.

### Copying the embed code
Click the `<>` icon to see the embed code for placing the survey on a page.

<img src="/images/guides/share-forms/share-embed-code.png" alt="The embed code icon highlighted in the survey link area of the share screen" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />
