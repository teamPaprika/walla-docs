## Standard view

- Every question is listed vertically on a single page, so respondents see them all at once.
- It's quick to scan the whole survey, but the page can get long when there are many questions.
## Grouped view

- Questions are shown one at a time, in order.
- Respondents click the `Next` button to move to the following question.
- Useful when you want respondents to focus on one question at a time.



## Showing several questions on one page

- ### Standard view + page breaks
  - Share in standard view and add `Page breaks` to split questions into sections that are comfortable to read at once.
  - A `Next` button appears at the bottom of each page so respondents move through the survey naturally.

- ### Grouped view + field groups
  - Bundle several questions into one field group, and grouped view will show all the questions in that group together on one page.
