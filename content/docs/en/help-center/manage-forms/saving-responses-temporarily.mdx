---
title: "Saving Responses Temporarily"
description: "When a respondent isn't able to finish a survey in one sitting, they can save their progress and come back to finish it later. There are two ways to use temporary saving."
---

## Automatic Temporary Saving

<img src="/images/guides/manage-forms/temporary-save-browser.png" alt="Save to browser enabled on the Responses tab of survey settings" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />

- Click the `⚙️` button at the top right and open the 'Response' tab.
- A respondent's inputs are automatically saved to their browser.
- Saved responses are kept for 15 days and then automatically deleted.
- Note: saving does not work in incognito (private) mode.
- If the respondent closes and reopens the survey, their previous inputs will still be there.

## Save Response by Signing In

<img src="/images/guides/manage-forms/temporary-save-login.png" alt="Save with login enabled on the Responses tab of survey settings" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />

- Click the `⚙️` button at the top right and open the 'Response' tab.
- Respondents can save progress while signed in with a Google account.
- To set it up:
  1. Enable 'Save progress when signed in'
  1. Enable 'Prevent duplicate responses'
  1. Enable 'Require email login'
- With this setup, signed-in users can stop mid-survey and resume later by logging back in with the same account.

<Callout title="Tip">
Automatic temporary saving is convenient but may be cleared depending on the browser. If you need reliable progress saving, the 'Save response by signing in' method is the safest choice.
</Callout>
