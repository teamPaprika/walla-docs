---
title: "Preventing Duplicate Responses"
description: "Stop the same person from submitting the same survey multiple times with the duplicate response prevention feature."
---

## How to Set It Up

1. In the project editor, click the `⚙️` button at the top right to go to the settings tab.
2. Turn on the 'Prevent duplicate responses' toggle.

## How It Works

- Participants who have already submitted a response cannot respond again even if they reopen the link.
- After submission, the survey page will not open — they'll instead see a message like "You've already submitted a response."

<Callout title="Tip">
Combining duplicate response prevention with the 'Require email login' feature lets you restrict responses by Google account, giving you even more accurate duplicate prevention.
</Callout>
