## How to View Activity Logs

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Go to the Activity Logs tab
    Navigate to Team Settings → 'Activity Logs' tab.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Review actions
    You can review the major actions taken across your team in chronological order.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Check log details
    Each log records who performed the action, when it happened, and what was done.
  </Step>
</Steps>
