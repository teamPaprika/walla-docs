## Blocking external AI use
Turning on `Block external AI use` stops this team's response data from being sent to external LLM providers such as OpenAI. Enabling this option disables the [Create a survey with AI](https://docs.walla.my/en/docs/help-center/create-forms/creating-project-ai) feature.
