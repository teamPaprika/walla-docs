## Blocking external AI use
Turning on `Block external AI usage` under `Team Management` → `Security` disables features that use external AI, including [Creating a Survey with AI](/en/docs/help-center/create-forms/creating-project-ai) and Walla AI chat, and prevents team response data from being sent to external LLM providers.

This setting operates independently of `Allow MCP (AI connector)` in [Team Security Settings](/en/docs/help-center/security/team-security-settings). Blocking external AI usage does not disable MCP integrations.
