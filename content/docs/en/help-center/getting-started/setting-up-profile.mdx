---
title: Setting Up Your Profile
description: Once you're logged in, click the round profile icon in the top-left corner to manage your account settings.
---

## Profile Settings

- Click the profile icon to see which account you're currently logged in with.
- Update your name or profile image to personalize your account.

## Login Session

- Set a login session duration and you'll be automatically logged out after the specified time.
- This feature is designed to improve security by automatically ending your session after a period of inactivity.

## Two-Factor Authentication (Extra Security)

- If you want to strengthen account security, enable two-factor authentication (2FA).
- 2FA adds an extra verification step at login to help keep your account safer.

## Personalized Notifications

- If you want to receive helpful news and updates from Walla, turn on 'Receive personalized notifications'.

## Language

- Choose the language you want to use in Walla.
- Regardless of this setting, respondents will always see the survey in the language that matches their browser settings.
