## Sign Up and Log In

- Sign up quickly by entering your email, name, and password.
- Once you've signed up, log in with the same email and password.

## Log In with Google

- On the login screen, click 'Sign in with Google'.
- Pick your Google account and you'll be logged in right away.

## Sign In with Company SSO

SSO (single sign-on) lets you sign in to Walla with the account you use at work. You can use it if your company has configured an SSO connection for Walla.

1. On the Walla login screen, click `Sign in with SSO`.
2. Enter your company email address and click `Continue`.
3. When your company's authentication screen opens, sign in with your company account. Follow the instructions if additional verification is required.
4. After authentication, you'll return to Walla. If this is your first visit, complete any registration steps shown.

<Callout type="info" title="Your company needs to configure SSO first">
Having a company email address alone does not enable SSO. Ask your administrator whether an SSO connection has been configured for your email domain.
</Callout>

### If You Can't Sign In with SSO

- Check your company email address for typos.
- If you see a message that SSO is not configured, or your company's authentication fails, ask your administrator to check the SSO connection and your account's access permissions.
- If you don't see the `Sign in with SSO` button, go back from any other login input screen to the screen where you choose a sign-in method. If the button still doesn't appear, ask your administrator whether SSO is supported in your environment.
