## Starting a Plan Subscription

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open the Team Management tab
    Go to Team Settings → `Team Management` tab.

    <img src="/images/guides/getting-started/team-management-tab.png" alt="The Team Management → Team Info menu in the team settings sidebar" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Click Change Plan
    Click the `Change Plan` button at the top.

    <img src="/images/guides/getting-started/plan-change-button.png" alt="The Change Plan button in the plan area of the Team Info screen" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose and apply your plan
    Review the pricing page, select the plan you want, and click `Apply Plan` to go to the payment page.

    <img src="/images/guides/getting-started/plan-selection-modal.png" alt="The plan selection modal comparing the Free, Pro, and Enterprise plans" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Enter your payment information
    Enter your email, card details, and other billing information to start your subscription. If you have a promotion code, enter it in `Add promotion code` to apply a discount at checkout.

    <img src="/images/guides/getting-started/payment-checkout.png" alt="The checkout page where card details are entered and the Subscribe button is clicked" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Note">
Walla uses an overseas payment gateway.
Before paying, make sure to disable the "block overseas KRW payments" setting in your card issuer's app. Some card issuers may also charge international payment fees.
</Callout>

## Cancelling a Plan

To cancel your current subscription, click `Cancel Plan`.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Click the Manage Subscription button on the Team Info page

    <img src="/images/guides/getting-started/subscription-management-buttons.png" alt="The Manage Subscription / Change Plan buttons on the Team Info screen of a team with a paid plan" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Click your active plan

    <img src="/images/guides/getting-started/stripe-billing-portal.png" alt="The Stripe billing portal showing the active plan, payment method, billing information, and invoice history" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Click Cancel Plan

    <img src="/images/guides/getting-started/stripe-plan-cancel-button.png" alt="The Update plan and Cancel plan buttons on the Pro plan detail screen" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Enter your cancellation reason and click Confirm Cancellation

    <img src="/images/guides/getting-started/stripe-cancel-reason-form.png" alt="The cancellation confirmation screen where a reason and extra details are entered before clicking Confirm cancellation" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Changing Your Payment Method

<Steps>
  <Step>

## Checking Payment Receipts

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Click the Manage Subscription button on the Team Info page

    <img src="/images/guides/getting-started/subscription-management-buttons.png" alt="The Manage Subscription / Change Plan buttons on the Team Info screen of a team with a paid plan" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Pick a billing date from the invoice history

    <img src="/images/guides/getting-started/stripe-invoice-history.png" alt="A receipt row showing billing date, amount, status, and plan highlighted in the invoice history section of the Stripe billing portal" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Download the invoice or receipt
Because Walla bills through an overseas payment gateway, if the amount on the invoice or receipt differs from what was actually charged, the difference may be your card issuer's international payment fee.
    <img src="/images/guides/getting-started/stripe-invoice-receipt-download.png" alt="The payment receipt page offering Download invoice and Download receipt buttons on a paid invoice screen" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="Warning">
- If overseas KRW payments are blocked at the time of billing, your payment may fail.
- Downgrading affects team members and response data, so make sure to back up your data and clean up storage before making changes.
- Before changing plans, please review each plan's details on the [pricing page](https://home.walla.my/en/pricing/main).
</Callout>
