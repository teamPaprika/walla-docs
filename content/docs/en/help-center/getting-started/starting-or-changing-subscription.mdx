## Starting a Subscription

1. Go to Team Settings → \[Team Management\] tab.
2. Click the \[Change Plan\] button at the top.
3. Review the pricing page and choose the plan you want.
4. Click \[Apply Plan\] to go to the payment page.
5. Enter your email, card info, and other billing details to start your subscription.

<Callout type="info" title="Note">
Walla uses an overseas payment gateway. Before paying, make sure to disable any "block overseas KRW payments" setting in your card issuer's app. Some card issuers may also charge international payment fees.
</Callout>

## Cancelling or Changing Your Plan

To change your current plan, click the \[Manage Plan\] button and choose the action you want from the popup.

- On the billing info page, you can review:
  - Your current plan
  - Payment method (card info, etc.)
  - Next billing date
  - `\[Update Subscription\] button` → **upgrade/downgrade plan**
  - `\[Cancel Subscription\] button` → **cancel subscription**

<Callout type="warn" title="Warning">
- If overseas KRW payments are blocked at the time of billing, your payment may fail.
- Downgrading affects team members and response data, so back up your data and clean up storage before making changes.
- Before changing plans, please review each plan's details on the [pricing page](https://home.walla.my/ko/pricing/main).
</Callout>
