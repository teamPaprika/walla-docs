---
title: Photo Capture and Video Capture Fields
description: Let respondents take a photo or record a video in real time and upload it directly, right from within the survey.
---

## Photo Capture Field

<img src="/images/guides/manage-forms/photo-capture-field-example.png" alt="A survey editor with a photo capture field applied" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />

- Respondents can open their device camera, take a photo, and upload it right away.
- The [text editor](https://docs.walla.my/en/docs/help-center/create-forms/editing-field-text#applying-text-styles) lets you apply various styles such as text color, size, and weight.
- You can also use [slash (/) commands](https://docs.walla.my/en/docs/help-center/create-forms/editing-field-text#using-slash--commands-for-more-features) to easily insert images, tables, headings, and more.
- Useful for profile pictures, on-site verification, supporting documentation, and more.
- Available options
  - Required
  - Data masking
  - Auto-delete responses

## Video Capture Field

<img src="/images/guides/manage-forms/video-capture-field-example.png" alt="A survey editor with a video capture field applied" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />

- Respondents can record and upload a video in real time.
- The [text editor](https://docs.walla.my/en/docs/help-center/create-forms/editing-field-text#applying-text-styles) lets you apply various styles such as text color, size, and weight.
- You can also use [slash (/) commands](https://docs.walla.my/en/docs/help-center/create-forms/editing-field-text#using-slash--commands-for-more-features) to easily insert images, tables, headings, and more.
- Great for video interviews, product reviews, and video-based feedback.
- Available options
  - Required
  - Data masking
  - Auto-delete responses
