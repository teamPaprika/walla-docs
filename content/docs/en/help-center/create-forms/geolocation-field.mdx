title: "Geolocation Field"
description: "A field that allows respondents to share their current location. Use this to collect real-world location data along with their responses."
---

## Geolocation Field

- Respondents click the `Get Location` button to share their current location.
- Available options
  - Required
  - Data masking
  - Auto-delete responses

<Callout type="warning" title="Warning">
Under privacy and location-information laws, you must obtain the user's consent before collecting location data. Use logic together with a consent field so that respondents can only fill out the geolocation field if they have agreed.
</Callout>
