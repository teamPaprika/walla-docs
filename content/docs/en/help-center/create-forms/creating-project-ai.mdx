## Creating a Survey with AI

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Click the + button
    On the workspace screen, click the `+` button.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Select Create Survey with AI
    Choose [Create Survey with AI].
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Enter your prompt
    Type the survey content you want as a prompt.

    <Callout title="Basic examples">
    - Please create a customer satisfaction survey.
    - Please generate a feedback survey about product experience.
    - Please create a user opinion survey for service improvement.
    </Callout>

    <Callout title="Examples with clear audience and purpose">
    - Please create a remote work satisfaction survey targeting office workers in their 20s.
    - Please generate a beta tester feedback survey before a new app launch.
    - Please create a course quality evaluation survey for online course students.
    </Callout>

    <Callout title="Examples with specific conditions">
    - Please create a survey with 5 multiple-choice questions and 2 open-ended questions.
    - Please generate a short survey that can be completed in under 3 minutes.
    - Please create a survey that collects an NPS score along with detailed feedback.
    </Callout>
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Review the preview
    Check the survey draft in the preview on the right. If you're not happy with the result, you can re-enter the prompt.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Generate the survey
    Once you like the draft, click [Generate] to create the survey.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Edit and refine
    Afterward, you can freely edit the survey.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Tip">
- The more specific your prompt, the more refined the resulting survey.
- To generate a survey in another language, simply request it in that language.
  - Example: "Please create a customer satisfaction survey in French."
</Callout>
