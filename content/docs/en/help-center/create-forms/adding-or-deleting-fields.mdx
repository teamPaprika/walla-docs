## Adding a Field

### Click the '+' icon in the left field list
Select the field you want and it will be added. If you add a field while another field is selected, the new one is automatically placed below it.

<img src="/images/guides/manage-forms/add-field-plus-icon.png" alt="The add field panel shown after clicking the + icon at the top of the field list on the left" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />

### Add from the middle of the edit screen
Hover the cursor between two fields and an `Add` button appears.
Click it to insert a new field at that position.

<img src="/images/guides/manage-forms/add-field-between.png" alt="The Add button that appears when hovering between fields in the editor" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />

### Add inside a field group
Hover the cursor at the bottom of a field group to reveal an `Add` button. Click it to add a question inside the group.

<img src="/images/guides/manage-forms/add-field-group.png" alt="The Add button that appears when hovering at the bottom of a field group" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />

## Deleting or Duplicating a Field
### Delete or duplicate directly in the editor
Use the icons on the right of a field to delete or duplicate the currently selected field.
<img src="/images/guides/manage-forms/field-action-icons.png" alt="The settings, duplicate, and delete icons shown in the top right of a field in the editor" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />

### Delete or duplicate from the field list
<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Find the more icon
    In the left field list, hover over a field and a `...` (more) icon appears on its right side.

    <img src="/images/guides/manage-forms/field-more-icon.png" alt="The more icon shown to the right of a field in the field list on the left" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose Delete or Duplicate
    Click the icon to choose either 'Delete' or 'Duplicate'.

    <img src="/images/guides/manage-forms/field-more-menu.png" alt="The duplicate field, save as field template, and delete field menu shown when clicking the more icon" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Caution">
Deleting a field also deletes all response data collected for that field. Be sure to back up any important data before deleting.
</Callout>
