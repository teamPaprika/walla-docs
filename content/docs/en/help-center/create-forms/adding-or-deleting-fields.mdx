## Adding a Field

### Click the '+' icon in the left field list
Select the field you want and it will be added. If you add a field while another field is selected, the new one is automatically placed below it.

### Add from the middle of the edit screen
Hover the cursor between two fields and an `Add` button appears.
Click it to insert a new field at that position.

### Add inside a field group
Hover the cursor at the bottom of a field group to reveal an `Add` button. Click it to add a question inside the group.

## Deleting or Duplicating a Field

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Hover over the field
    In the left field list, hover your mouse over the field.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Find the more icon
    A `...` (more) icon appears on the right side of the field.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose Delete or Duplicate
    Click the icon to choose either 'Delete' or 'Duplicate'.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Caution">
Deleting a field also deletes all response data collected for that field. Be sure to back up any important data before deleting.
</Callout>
