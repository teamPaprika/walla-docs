## Importing a Template

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Visit the templates page
    Go to the [templates page](https://home.walla.my/template-kr).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose a template
    Pick the template you like, then click 'Use Now'.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Select a team and workspace
    Choose the team and workspace to import the template into, then click \[Duplicate Template\].
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Done
    You've successfully imported the template.
  </Step>
</Steps>
