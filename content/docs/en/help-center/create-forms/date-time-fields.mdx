## Date Field

<img src="/images/guides/manage-forms/date-field-example.png" alt="A survey editor with a date field applied" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />

- Allows respondents to choose a date.
- The [text editor](https://docs.walla.my/en/docs/help-center/create-forms/editing-field-text#applying-text-styles) lets you apply various styles such as text color, size, and weight.
- You can also use [slash (/) commands](https://docs.walla.my/en/docs/help-center/create-forms/editing-field-text#using-slash--commands-for-more-features) to easily insert images, tables, headings, and more.
- Available options
  - Required
  - Data masking
  - Auto-delete responses

## Time Field

<img src="/images/guides/manage-forms/time-field-example.png" alt="A survey editor with a time field applied" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />

- Allows respondents to choose a time.
- The [text editor](https://docs.walla.my/en/docs/help-center/create-forms/editing-field-text#applying-text-styles) lets you apply various styles such as text color, size, and weight.
- You can also use [slash (/) commands](https://docs.walla.my/en/docs/help-center/create-forms/editing-field-text#using-slash--commands-for-more-features) to easily insert images, tables, headings, and more.
- Available options
  - Required
  - Data masking
  - Auto-delete responses

<Callout title="Tip">
Date and time fields are useful when building time-related surveys such as booking requests, scheduling, or event sign-ups.
</Callout>
