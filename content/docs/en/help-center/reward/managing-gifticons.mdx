---
title: "Managing Gifticons"
description: "In Walla, you can send gifticons (rewards) to survey respondents. Once sent, you can check and manage them on the reward management page."
---

## What You Can See
- Check the status of each item right away so you can quickly spot any missing or failed deliveries.
  - Reward issue date
  - Delivery status (including success / failure)
  - Recipient phone number
  - Reward type and quantity sent

## Search and Filter
- Use the search bar at the top to search by respondent name, phone number, or other keywords.
- Filter by response period, reward delivery status, or response conditions to narrow down the list.
