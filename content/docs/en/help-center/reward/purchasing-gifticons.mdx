---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

## How to Purchase Rewards

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Go to the Reward Management page
    Navigate to the `Reward Management` page.

    <img src="/images/guides/reward/step-01-reward-management.png" alt="The Reward Management page" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)"}} />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Click Buy Reward
    Click the `Buy Reward` button at the top.

    <img src="/images/guides/reward/step-02-purchase-button.png" alt="The Buy Reward button" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)"}} />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose a reward
    Pick the reward you want from the available brand list (e.g., Starbucks, Baedal Minjok, etc.).

    <img src="/images/guides/reward/step-03-reward-select.png" alt="Choosing a reward" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)"}} />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose quantity and pay
    Select the reward type and quantity, then proceed to payment.

    <img src="/images/guides/reward/step-04-payment.png" alt="Confirming the payment" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)"}} />

    <img src="/images/guides/reward/step-04-payment-card.png" alt="Entering payment details" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)"}} />

    <Callout type="warning" title="Warning">
    - Walla uses an overseas Payment Gateway, so overseas transaction fees may apply depending on your card issuer.
    - Before paying, be sure to disable the 'Block overseas KRW payments' (DCC block) setting in your card issuer's app or website. (Payment will fail if this setting is enabled.)
    </Callout>
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Purchase complete
    Once payment is processed, you'll see a confirmation that the reward purchase is complete.

    <img src="/images/guides/reward/step-05-purchase-complete.png" alt="Purchase complete" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)"}} />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Send rewards
    From the reward management page, review your purchase or send rewards to your respondents.
  </Step>
</Steps>
