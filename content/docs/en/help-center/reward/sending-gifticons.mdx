title: "Sending Gifticons"
description: "In Walla, you can send gifticons (rewards) to your survey respondents. Once you've purchased rewards, you're ready to send them out."
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

## Steps to Send Rewards

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Go to the Reward Management page
    Navigate to the `Reward Management` page.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Click Send Reward
    Under 'My Rewards', click the `Send Reward` button.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Select respondents
    Choose the question that contains the recipient's contact info (phone number field). You can pick respondents manually, or click `Random Pick` to select them at random.

    <Callout type="warning" title="Warning">
    Rewards cannot be sent to respondents whose phone number format is invalid.
    </Callout>
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Check eligibility
    Review the selected respondents and exclude those who have already received a reward.
    - You can see at a glance who received which rewards, and how many times.
    - You can also go back to the previous step to reselect respondents.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Write a message
    Write a message to send along with the reward.
    - Title: up to 40 bytes
    - Body: up to 200 characters

    The message will be shown to respondents when they receive their reward.

    <Callout type="warning" title="Warning">
    - Rewards are sent to the phone number entered in the phone number field.
    - Once sent, rewards cannot be cancelled or retrieved.
    - Clicking the Send Reward button is considered acceptance of all terms.
    </Callout>
  </Step>
</Steps>
