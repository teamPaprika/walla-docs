## How to Connect

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open form settings
    Click the settings (gear) button in the top right of the form editor to open form settings.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Go to the External Tracking tab
    In the form settings window, select the 'External Tracking' tab from the left menu.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Enter your measurement IDs
    Enter the ID for the platform you want to track. Your entries are saved automatically.
    - Meta Pixel ID: e.g., 123456789012345
    - GA4 Measurement ID: e.g., G-XXXXXXXXXX
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Events Sent

Once connected, the following standard events are sent to each platform based on respondent activity.

- On form view: PageView (Meta) / page_view (GA4)
- On submission: Lead (Meta) / generate_lead (GA4)

Conversion events include the walla_response_id and walla_customer_key parameters. When setting up an ad conversion goal, select these standard event names.
