## How to Connect

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Go to the Automation tab
    In the project editor, open the 'Automation' tab.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Click Connect
    Under 'Google Sheets', click `Connect`.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Sign in with Google
    Click `Sign in with Google` and log in with your Google account.
    - All Google accounts available in this workspace will be shown.
    - You can pick an existing account or sign in with a new one.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Sheet created
    Once connected, Walla automatically creates a Google Sheet (owned by you and saved to your Drive). You can copy the sheet's link to open it.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Disconnect (if needed)
    To disconnect, click the `Disconnect` button.
  </Step>
</Steps>
## How It Works
- When a new response is submitted, each response field is automatically mapped to a column in the sheet.
- Data syncs in real time, so you can see responses immediately without any manual export.
<Callout type="warning" title="Warning">
The connected Google account must have edit access to the sheet. If the sheet is shared, make sure it has edit access as well.
</Callout>
