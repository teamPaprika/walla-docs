## How to Set It Up

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Pick Zapier and log in
    In the 'Connect' menu, choose Zapier and log in.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Select Walla Form
    In the App & event step, select 'Walla Form'.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Set the Event
    Set the Event to 'New Response'.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Sign in with your Walla account
    Under Account, log in with your Walla account.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Pick a project
    Choose the project you want to receive response notifications from.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Connect an external service
    In the next Account step, pick the external service to connect to. (e.g., Google Sheets, Slack, etc.)
  </Step>
</Steps>


<Callout type="warning" title="Heads up when connecting to Google Sheets">
To sync with Google Sheets through Zapier, the first row of your sheet must contain headers (column names). Walla responses will only be recorded properly when those headers exist.
</Callout>
