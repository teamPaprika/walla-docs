Users with the **Add members** permission enabled in a workspace (by default, Owners and Admins) can add or invite new members from the **Invite** tab by clicking the gear icon (**Workspace Settings**) at the top right of the workspace.

## Adding Existing Team Members · Inviting via Email

On the **Invite → Invite** tab, you can immediately add team members who have already joined your team, or send email invitations to people who are not yet on the team.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Select recipients and assign a role

    Select the team members to add from the list, choose the **Workspace role** to apply from the dropdown at the bottom right (defaults to **Unassigned** on Enterprise, **Member** on Free · Pro), and click **Add**. For email addresses not yet in the team, use the **Email invite** input field to send invitations at once.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Confirm member addition

    Once added, the new member appears immediately in the **Member Management** list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Inviting with an Invite Link

On the **Invite → Invite link** tab, you can pre-configure a **Link expiry** and **Workspace role** to create multiple invite links and manage them in a list.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Choose expiry & role and create an invite link

    Select the **Link expiry** (for example, `7 days`) and the **Workspace role** to assign upon joining, then click **Create invite link**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Copy the created invite link

    The new link is added to the **Created invite links** list below. Click the link to copy the URL and share it with the people you want to invite.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Change the expiry

    Open the **Expiry** dropdown on a created link to adjust its remaining validity period directly.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Delete an invite link

    For links you no longer need, click the delete icon on the right side of the row to invalidate them immediately. Click **Confirm** in the confirmation dialog to delete the link and refresh the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Sent Invites

On the **Invite → Sent invites** tab, you can review pending email invitations and their expiration status, copy invite links, resend invitations, or cancel invitations.
