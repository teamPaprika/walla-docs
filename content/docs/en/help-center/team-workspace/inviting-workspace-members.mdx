## Before You Start

- Only members who have already joined the team can be invited to a workspace. For people outside the team, start with [Inviting Team Members](/help-center/team-workspace/inviting-team-members).
- Workspace invitations are available on the **Pro plan or higher**. (On the Free plan, team-level invitations themselves are not supported.)

## Inviting Members to a Workspace

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open Workspace Settings
    In the left sidebar, click the gear icon next to the workspace you want to invite people to, opening Workspace Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Go to the Member Management Tab
    In the settings modal, switch to the `Member Management` tab.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Select Team Members and Add Them
    Pick from the list of team members who have already joined the team, then add them. The selected members are joined to the workspace immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Assigning Roles">
For workspace roles (Admin/Member, etc.) and permission management, see the [Managing Workspaces](/help-center/team-workspace/managing-workspaces#roles-and-permissions) page.
</Callout>

## Removing Members

- Use the menu next to each member in the `Member Management` tab to remove them from the workspace.
- A member removed from a workspace no longer has access to that workspace's projects but remains part of the team itself.
