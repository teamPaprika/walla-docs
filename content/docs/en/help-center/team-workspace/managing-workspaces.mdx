You can manage **Member Management**, **Invitations**, and **Roles & Permissions** within a workspace in one place by clicking the gear icon (**Workspace Settings**) in the top right of the workspace screen.

## Managing Members and Roles

### Member Management

On the **Member Management** tab, you can view the list of members currently in the workspace along with each member's role, and use the role dropdown or more (`⋯`) menu to change roles or remove a member from the workspace.

To add existing team members or invite someone via email or invite link, use the **Invite** tab (**Invite link**, **Invite**, **Sent invites**) in the left menu. For detailed instructions, see [Inviting Workspace Members](/en/docs/help-center/team-workspace/inviting-workspace-members).

### Roles & Permissions

On the **Roles & Permissions** tab, configure **Projects** (10 permissions), **Privacy** (3 permissions), and **Advanced** (4 permissions) for each role. On Free and Pro plans, Admins and Members share identical workspace permissions; on the **Enterprise plan**, you can use **Add role**, the **Unassigned** role, per-role permission customization, and **Follow project settings** delegation.

When you select any role and switch to the **Member list** tab at the top, you can view the members assigned to that role and click **Add member** to assign the role to additional members right away.

For a complete explanation of the role hierarchy and all 17 detailed permissions, see [Managing Permissions](/en/docs/help-center/security/managing-permissions).

## Deleting a Workspace and Transferring Ownership

Deleting a workspace or transferring its ownership is controlled by the **Owner** role and the Team Management menu.

### Deleting a Workspace

- **Bottom of the Workspace Settings modal**: Click **Delete workspace** at the bottom of the left menu in Workspace Settings. *(Regular members see **Leave workspace** instead.)*
- **Team Management page**: In the left sidebar under **Team Management → Manage Workspaces**, open the more (`⋯`) menu on the right side of the workspace row and select **Delete**.

<Callout type="warn" title="Warning">
Deleting a workspace permanently removes all projects, response data, and invite links inside it and cannot be undone.
</Callout>

### Transferring Ownership

To transfer ownership from the current owner to another team member, go to **Team Management → Manage Workspaces**, open the more (`⋯`) menu on the workspace row, and select **Transfer ownership**.
