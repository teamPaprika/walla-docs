## Member Management (Invitations)

- Click 'Member Management' to invite team members to the workspace.
- Workspace invitations are not available on the Free plan since team member invitations are disabled.

## Roles and Permissions

- Roles are divided into Admin and Member.
- For each role, you can allow or restrict access to the following features.
<Callout title="Features You Can Control with Permissions">
- Edit roles and permissions: create/edit roles and change permissions
- Assign roles: assign roles to members
- Add/remove members: invite or remove workspace members
- Manage projects: move, duplicate, delete projects
- Edit projects: edit project content
- View responses: access response pages
- Delete responses: delete collected responses
- Download responses: export response data
- Edit masking options: use masking options when editing projects
- Restrict masked data access: control who can view masked responses
- Auto-delete response settings: set auto-delete retention periods
</Callout>

## Deleting a Workspace
  
  - Click 'Delete Workspace' to remove the workspace.
  - You can also do this from 'Team Settings' at the bottom left.

<Callout type="warn" title="Warning">
Deleting a workspace cannot be undone, so we recommend backing up any important data first.
</Callout>

## Changing the Workspace Owner

- Under 'Team Management → Manage Workspaces' in the top left, you can see the list of workspaces in your team and change the owner of each one.
