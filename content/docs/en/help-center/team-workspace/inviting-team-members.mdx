## Before You Start

- Team member invitations are available starting from the **Pro plan**.
- You need **Owner** or **Admin** permissions to send invitations.

## Sending Invitations by Email

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open Team Settings
    Click the team name in the top menu, go to `Team Settings`, and open the `Team Members` tab.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Enter Email Addresses
    In the invite area at the top of the screen, enter the new member's email address. You can enter multiple emails at once to invite several people in a single batch.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose a Role and Send
    Select the role (Admin, Member, etc.) for the invitee and click `Send Invitation`. An invitation email is sent to each address you entered.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="You can change roles later">
The role chosen at invitation time can be updated anytime from the `Team Members` tab once the person joins. For a full breakdown of roles and permissions, see the [Managing Your Team](/help-center/team-workspace/managing-your-team) page.
</Callout>

## Inviting via Invite Link

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Create an Invite Link
    In the `Invite Link` area, click the **Create Invite Link** button to generate a new link.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Copy and Share the Link
    Click the copy icon next to the generated link, then send it to anyone you want to add. By default, the link is valid for 7 days.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Change the Expiry
    Click the **Change Expiry** button to the right of the link to set a new remaining lifetime in hours.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Confirm the Updated Expiry
    The expiry updates immediately based on the value you set, and the new expiration time is shown below the link.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Delete the Invite Link
    When the link is no longer needed, click the trash icon to delete it. The link becomes invalid right away, but members who already joined remain on the team.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Checking and Managing Invitations

- Invitations that haven't been accepted yet appear in the member list of the `Team Members` tab with a **Pending** status.
- The menu next to each pending invitation lets you:
  - **Resend invitation**: Send the email again if it was missed or has expired.
  - **Cancel invitation**: Immediately expire an invitation that was sent by mistake or is no longer needed.

<Callout type="warning" title="Note">
Cancelling an invitation does not recall the email itself, but the cancelled link will no longer work.
</Callout>

## How the Invitee Accepts

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Check the Invitation Email
    The invitation email includes the team name, the inviter, and an accept button.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Click the Accept Link
    Clicking `Accept Invitation` opens the Walla sign-up/sign-in screen. If they already have an account, signing in is enough.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Join the Team
    After signing in, the invitee is automatically added to the team with the role chosen at invitation time.
  </Step>
</Steps>
