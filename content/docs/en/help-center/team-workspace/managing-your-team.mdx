## Team Info

<img src="/images/guides/team-workspace/team-info-page.png" alt="Reviewing basic team information and plan details on the Team Management → Team Info screen" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />

- See basic information about the team you're currently in (team profile image, team name, owner info, etc.).
- Your subscribed plan, total/remaining storage, and member count are also displayed.
- You can view the list of workspaces in your team and change each workspace's owner or delete it.

## Workspace Management

<img src="/images/guides/team-workspace/workspace-management-page.png" alt="The workspace list with the transfer ownership and delete workspace menu" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />

- View information about all workspaces created in the team.
- See how many members each workspace has and who owns it, transfer workspace ownership, or delete workspaces.

## Team Member Management

<img src="/images/guides/team-workspace/team-member-management.png" alt="The team member management screen showing the invite area and the owner, admin, and member lists" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />

- Invitations are available starting from the Pro plan.
- View the full list of members in your team.
- Assign roles and manage permissions for each member.
- Transfer team ownership or remove members from this tab.
### Managing team permissions

Team roles rank as Owner > Admin > Member.
The team owner can toggle detailed permissions for the **Admin** and **Member** roles in the `Role permissions` section at the bottom of the page.

<img src="/images/guides/team-workspace/team-roles-admin.png" alt="The Admin tab of role permissions with toggles enabled for inviting members, creating workspaces, removing members, changing roles, security settings, and downloading logs" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />

## Security Settings

<img src="/images/guides/team-workspace/security-settings.png" alt="The team security settings screen with two-factor authentication, IP address verification, and require reason for response download" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />

- Provides features for strengthening team access security.
  - **Two-Factor Authentication (MFA)**: Force team members to complete additional verification (OTP, etc.) at login.
  - **IP Address Verification**: Restrict team access to registered IP addresses or ranges.
  - **Require reason for response download**: Require users to enter a reason when downloading response data.

## Activity Logs

<img src="/images/guides/team-workspace/activity-logs.png" alt="The team activity log screen organized into timestamp, user, action, description, and metadata columns" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", marginTop: "12px", marginBottom: "12px", border: "1px solid var(--border)" }} />

- Review all major team activity in chronological order.
- Click `Download CSV` to export the activity logs.
